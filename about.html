Who We Are

Santa Express Services Sdn Bhd is a Brunei-based service provider specialising in oilfield support, logistics, transportation, manpower supply and industrial solutions.

With strong dedication towards safety, reliability and operational excellence, we support clients by providing efficient solutions that meet industry requirements.

Our experienced team works closely with customers to deliver high-quality services while maintaining professional standards.

Our Vision

To become a trusted and leading service provider in Brunei's energy and industrial sectors through quality, innovation and reliability.

Our Mission
